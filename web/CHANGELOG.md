# Sarala for the web

The web app has its own version, apart from the desktop app. Each section here
becomes the notes of a `web-vX.Y.Z` release.

## 0.1.0

The first public preview of Sarala in the browser.

- Live Markdown editing with math, diagrams, code highlighting, tables and voice typing, all running on your device.
- No account and no server. Documents stay in this browser, or in real folders on your device in Chrome and Edge.
- Works offline once loaded, and installs as an app.
- Import and export with Word, PDF, HTML and more.
- A new look of its own, with a readable page and a book typeface.
- Very long documents stay fast: tens of thousands of lines open quickly and scroll smoothly, and a single huge table or list no longer slows typing.

Not yet tried on a real iPhone, in real Safari or with a screen reader.
